## Where to Start (and What to Skip)

### This Week (1–2 hours)

1. **Pick your role** from the Series Index above. If your title spans multiple (security + platform, for instance), pick the one where your next promotion lives.
2. **Read Post 1: The Foundation.** It covers the shared vocabulary (transformers, embeddings, RAG, agents, evaluation) that every subsequent post assumes you know.

### This Month (10–15 hours)

1. **Read your role-specific post.** Each post is a self-contained landscape map with graded resources, maturity assessments, and a recommended learning sequence.
2. **Execute the "This Week" recommendations from that post.** Each one identifies the single most valuable tool to install and the single most valuable resource to read for immediate impact.

### This Quarter (ongoing)

1. **Work through the curated resources in your role post.** They're sequenced from beginner to advanced, with time estimates. A full quarter gets you from "aware of the landscape" to "executing within it."
2. **Cross-reference one adjacent role.** Security engineers should skim the Platform post (model serving has security implications). Data engineers should skim the Developer post (RAG pipelines bridge both territories). Leaders should skim everything.

### What to Skip

- **Generic "learn AI" courses** that treat every role the same. The territory has already fragmented. These courses teach the geography of a country that no longer exists.
- **$3,000 university AI certificates.** The ROI rarely justifies the cost. The same knowledge is available through the curated resources in this series for the cost of your time.
- **Fine-tuning tutorials** (for most roles). Unless you're a platform or ML engineer, fine-tuning is almost certainly not your highest-leverage skill. RAG, evaluation, and prompt engineering cover 90% of production use cases.
- **"Build GPT from scratch" courses.** Understanding transformer architecture at a conceptual level matters (covered in the Foundation post). Implementing backpropagation from scratch does not help you ship AI-augmented features in your role.
- **Any resource older than 12 months** that hasn't been updated. This space moves fast enough that a 2024 "State of AI tools" guide is archaeology, not guidance.
